About

I'm Rohan — an undergrad at IIT Dhanbad, and an aspiring filmmaker, video editor, and cinematographer.

I picked up a camera out of curiosity, but stayed for the stories it could tell. What began as filming moments around campus slowly turned into an obsession with framing emotions, pacing edits, and creating visuals that leave a lasting feeling.

Today, I work across cinematography, editing, and visual storytelling — creating everything from cinematic travel films and event aftermovies to promotional content and personal passion projects. Every frame I shoot and every cut I make is driven by one goal: to tell stories that people don't just watch, but feel.

Based in Dhanbad, India
Studying at IIT Dhanbad
Focus Cinematography · Editing
Tools Premiere Pro · DaVinci Resolve · After Effects
My process

For the past three years, I've been making films and editing stories — constantly experimenting with what makes a moment feel right.

My style is cinematic, story-driven, and detail-focused. I care about the feeling behind a shot just as much as the shot itself. I build around music, pacing, composition, and those small details that make an edit feel alive.

I don't just cut clips together. I try to make the viewer feel something.
